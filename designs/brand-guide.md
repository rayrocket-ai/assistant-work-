# The Unseen Edit — Brand Guide (V1)

Faceless YouTube channel: investigative deep dives into makeup brands.
Tagline: **"Every brand has a story they don't want told."**
Full visual concept package with mockups: [`the-unseen-edit-concepts.html`](./the-unseen-edit-concepts.html)

## Positioning

Intersection of three proven formats:

| Lane | Reference channels | What we borrow |
|---|---|---|
| Faceless business documentary | MagnatesMedia, ColdFusion, Company Man | Dark cinematic thumbnails, giant condensed caps, "dark side of ___" flagship formula |
| Investigative exposé | Coffeezilla, Fern, Lemmino | Evidence-first editing, receipts on screen, restrained two-color palette, grain |
| Beauty commentary | Cruel World Happy Mind, Spill Sesh, Smokey Glow | Products framed as exhibits, screenshot "receipts" grammar |
| Conspiracy-board vernacular | True-crime title cards, evidence walls | Manila folders, typewriter type, red string, redaction bars, CLASSIFIED stamps |

## The brand idea: concealer as censorship

Makeup conceals; the channel un-conceals. The signature device is **the redaction bar drawn
as a lipstick swipe** — it redacts "UNSEEN" in the wordmark, hides brand names in thumbnails,
and wipes across the screen as the channel transition. One device, three jobs: logo,
thumbnail hook, edit language.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Darkroom | `#100D0B` | Primary ground (warm near-black) |
| File Paper | `#EFE7DA` | Headline text on dark; document surfaces |
| Foundation Manila | `#C9A87C` | Folders, chapter cards — the makeup-world neutral |
| Pigment Red | `#E8112D` | The swipe, string, stamps, REC dot — ≤10% of any frame |
| Smoke | `#8D8377` | Captions, metadata, timecodes |

Frame ratio: **60% darkroom · 20% paper · 12% manila · 8% red.**

## Typography (all Google Fonts, free commercial use)

- **Oswald 700/500** — display: thumbnails, titles, headlines. All caps, tight leading.
- **Courier Prime** — evidence voice: stamps, captions, timecodes, receipts.
- **Source Serif 4** — narrator voice: descriptions, community posts.
- Substitutes: Bebas Neue / Special Elite / Lora.

## Logo suite

- **A — Primary wordmark:** THE UNSEEN EDIT with "UNSEEN" struck by the red swipe.
- **B — Avatar:** "UE" monogram in a compact-mirror circle with the swipe; reads at 48px.
- **C — Stamp lockup:** typewriter case-file stamp for paper surfaces, always slightly rotated.

## Thumbnail system (four series, fixed templates)

1. **S1 · The Dark Side Of ___** — flagship monthly deep dive. Black ground, giant caps,
   brand name inside the redaction swipe, one spotlit product.
2. **S2 · Case File** — scandal histories. Manila folder, typewriter title, CLASSIFIED
   stamp, pinned polaroid, red string. Use money figures in titles.
3. **S3 · The Receipts** — screenshot stories. Tilted screenshots, highlighter,
   red circle, two-word verdict headline.
4. **S4 · The Formula** — ingredient investigations. Ingredient list with one line
   redacted under a magnifier; warning tape.

**Rules:** ≤4 words per headline · exactly one red element · 8–10% film grain on
everything · mono episode chip bottom-left (`EP. 014`, red left border).

## Channel art

- Banner 2560×1440, all content inside the 1546×423 TV-safe area: wordmark + tagline
  "Makeup's money, myths & buried stories" + schedule line.
- Handle: `@theunseenedit`. Watermark: avatar at 50% opacity, bottom-right, from 0:30.

## In-video package

Cold-open title card (brand name arrives redacted, then swipes away) · source-tag lower
thirds on every claim · manila chapter cards ("Part Two", VERIFIED stamp) · "CASE CLOSED"
end screen with Next Case / Start Here slots.

**Motion:** nothing floats — snap, stamp, swipe in 2–6 frames with hard sounds
(typewriter clack, stamp thud). No smooth bouncy easing.

## Guardrails

Never: shocked-face clickbait grammar, more than one red element, beauty-pastels or
script fonts, decorative red. Always: five colors only, a receipt on screen for every
claim, fictional brands in promo mockups (real names only inside evidenced videos),
serialize everything with case numbers.
