# The Unseen Edit — Brand Guide (V2, "Paper Edition")

Faceless YouTube channel: investigative deep dives into makeup brands.
Tagline: **"Every brand has a story they don't want told."**
Full visual concept package with mockups: [`the-unseen-edit-concepts.html`](./the-unseen-edit-concepts.html)

**V2 changes:** the channel's home surfaces flip from dark to daylight file paper; the
stamp lockup is promoted to the primary logo and expanded into a four-mark suite; the
thumbnail system is locked unchanged from V1 (approved).

## Positioning

Intersection of three proven formats:

| Lane | Reference channels | What we borrow |
|---|---|---|
| Faceless business documentary | MagnatesMedia, ColdFusion, Company Man | Dark cinematic thumbnails, giant condensed caps, "dark side of ___" flagship formula |
| Investigative exposé | Coffeezilla, Fern, Lemmino | Evidence-first editing, receipts on screen, restrained palette, grain |
| Beauty commentary | Cruel World Happy Mind, Spill Sesh, Smokey Glow | Products framed as exhibits, screenshot "receipts" grammar |
| Conspiracy-board vernacular | True-crime title cards, evidence walls | Manila folders, typewriter type, red string, redaction bars, rubber stamps |

## The brand idea: the file room, in daylight

Makeup conceals; the channel un-conceals. The identity leads with the physical world of
the investigation — **paper, folders, and the rubber stamp**. Dark frames are rationed to
where they earn clicks: thumbnails and footage. The **lipstick-swipe redaction bar**
stays as the secondary device (hides brand names, acts as the transition).
Stamp for identity, swipe for reveals.

## Logo — the stamp suite (primary mark)

Set in Courier Prime. Always rotated −2° to −4°, always 10–15% distressed — never
straight, never vector-clean.

- **A — Primary stamp:** double border (9px outer / 2.5px inner), "CASE FILE —
  CONFIDENTIAL" header, "THE UNSEEN EDIT" main line, dateline rule, swappable footer
  ("DO NOT CIRCULATE" / "EVIDENCE ENCLOSED" / "CASE № 014"). Banner, end cards, posts, merch.
- **B — Round seal:** circular stamp, "THE UNSEEN EDIT · EST. 2026 · CASE FILES ·"
  around a "UE" center. Avatar (reads at 48px) and in-video watermark at 40% opacity.
- **C — Reverse stamp:** paper-ink (`#EFE8DB`) version for dark surfaces/footage;
  compact one-line cut for tight corners.
- **D — Folder stamp:** deep red (`#9C0A1D`) for manila surfaces (chapter cards,
  Case File thumbnails), with a case-number slot.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| File Paper | `#EFE8DB` | **Primary ground:** channel art, cards, posts, documents |
| Foundation Manila | `#C9A87C` | Folders, chapter cards — the makeup-world neutral |
| Ink | `#231B12` | All reading text on paper |
| Stamp Red | `#C40E24` | Stamps, string, circles on paper (brighter `#E8112D` for the swipe on dark) — ≤10% of any frame |
| Darkroom | `#100D0B` | Thumbnails & footage grade **only** — never the channel's resting state |

Channel-surface ratio: **55% paper · 18% manila · 15% ink · 8% red · 4% dark.**
Thumbnails deliberately invert this — dark frames pop against the bright channel page.

## Typography (all Google Fonts, free commercial use)

- **Courier Prime** — the identity face: logo/stamps, captions, timecodes, receipts.
- **Oswald 700/500** — display: thumbnails, titles, headlines. All caps, tight leading.
- **Source Serif 4** — narrator voice: descriptions, community posts.
- Substitutes: Special Elite / Bebas Neue / Lora.

## Thumbnail system (locked from V1 — approved)

1. **S1 · The Dark Side Of ___** — flagship monthly deep dive. Black ground, giant caps,
   brand name inside the redaction swipe, one spotlit product.
2. **S2 · Case File** — scandal histories. Manila folder, typewriter title, CLASSIFIED
   stamp, pinned polaroid, red string. Use money figures in titles.
3. **S3 · The Receipts** — screenshot stories. Tilted screenshots, highlighter,
   red circle, two-word verdict headline.
4. **S4 · The Formula** — ingredient investigations. Ingredient list with one line
   redacted under a magnifier; warning tape.

**Rules:** ≤4 words per headline · exactly one red element · 8–10% film grain on
everything · mono episode chip bottom-left (`EP. 014`, red left border).

  area; red string + typewriter corner notes as desktop garnish.
- Avatar: round seal. Handle: `@theunseenedit`. Watermark from 0:30.

## In-video package

Interstitials are paper documents: cold-open title card (title types onto a document,
CASE OPEN stamp thuds in, brand name arrives redacted then swipes away) · paper
source-tag lower thirds on every claim · manila chapter cards (VERIFIED stamp) ·
"CASE CLOSED" end screen — two video slots as photo prints on the desk.

**Motion:** nothing floats — snap, stamp, swipe in 2–6 frames with hard sounds
(typewriter clack, stamp thud). No smooth bouncy easing.

## Guardrails

Never: shocked-face clickbait grammar, more than one red element per thumbnail, a clean
straight stamp, beauty-pastels or script fonts. Always: channel surfaces on paper,
imperfect stamping, a receipt on screen for every claim, fictional brands in promo
mockups, serialize everything with case numbers.
