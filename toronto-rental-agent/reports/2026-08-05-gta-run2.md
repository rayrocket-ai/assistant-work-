### Caveats
- 10% minimum down requires living in one unit (3-4 unit insured rules);
  pure rentals need 20%.
- Oshawa numbers use single (provincial) land transfer tax — a real ~$14k
  closing-cost advantage vs Toronto at this price point.
- Tenanted units: Ontario rent control means inherited rents can sit far below
  market — always verify the rent roll.
